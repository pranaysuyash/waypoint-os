Where it fits

Being specific here saves us both time.

A good fit if

  • You run a boutique or specialist agency, not a call centre.
  • Requests arrive as messy threads across WhatsApp, email and calls.
  • Two to twenty people need the same context on the same trip.
  • You re-type the same details into proposals, supplier emails and vouchers.

Not a good fit if

  • You need a consumer-facing booking engine.
  • You want a CRM-first pipeline with marketing automation attached.
  • Your requests already arrive structured and complete.
  • You’re looking for an AI that quotes on your behalf without review.
