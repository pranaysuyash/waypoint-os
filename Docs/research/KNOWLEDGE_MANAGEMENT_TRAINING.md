## Purpose

Investigate how knowledge flows between team members in multi-agent agencies — how trip templates get created and reused, how new agents learn an agency's preferred suppliers and pricing approach, and how the system can encode and propagate agency-specific knowledge rather than processing each trip in isolation.

---

## Key Questions

- How do trip templates get created, shared, and reused across agents?
- How does an agency's "way of doing things" get encoded into the system?
- How does a junior agent learn from a senior agent's past itineraries and decisions?
- What knowledge should be shared agency-wide vs kept agent-private?
- How do we capture tribal knowledge before it leaves with a departing agent?
- How does the system train new agents on agency-specific workflows?
- What knowledge decay and freshness signals matter?
- How do we balance template reuse with trip customization?

---

## Research Areas

- Template creation and sharing workflows (itinerary templates, email templates, quote templates)
- Cross-agent knowledge capture from override patterns and corrections
- Agency-specific playbook creation and maintenance
- Agent training acceleration via AI-assisted guidance and coaching
- Knowledge decay and freshness signals (when does a supplier rate or preference become stale?)
- Successful itinerary repository with search and similarity matching
- Role-specific knowledge views (what does a junior need vs a senior vs an owner?)
- Internal wiki / knowledge base integration patterns
