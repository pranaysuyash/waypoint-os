## Purpose

Design the active-travel monitoring, crisis communication, and emergency response capabilities that transform the system from a pre-trip planning tool into a full-service travel management platform. Corporate travel clients increasingly require duty-of-care compliance, making this both a product differentiator and a commercial requirement.

---

## Key Questions

- How do we monitor active trips for disruptions? (flight status, weather, local events)
- How do we automate rebooking on flight cancellations or delays?
- How does the system communicate with travelers during an emergency?
- What duty-of-care requirements exist for corporate travel clients?
- How do we handle medical emergencies and insurance coordination?
- What is the escalation path when automated recovery is insufficient?
- How do we track traveler location without violating privacy?
- How do we handle multi-channel crisis communication? (WhatsApp, SMS, email, phone)
- What's the role of human operators in crisis vs automated response?

---

## Research Areas

- Real-time trip monitoring: flight status APIs (FlightAware, FlightStats), weather alerts, security feeds
- Automated disruption recovery: rebooking logic, re-routing, hotel rebooking, transfer adjustment
- Crisis communication: multi-channel alerts (WhatsApp, SMS, email, phone), templated messages
- Traveler safety status tracking and check-in workflow
- Duty-of-care compliance for corporate travel programs
- Medical emergency handling: insurance verification, hospital coordination, repatriation
- Privacy-preserving location tracking and data retention policies
- Incident response playbooks and escalation triggers
- Extraction logistics for high-risk scenarios (natural disasters, political unrest)
- Integration with emergency assistance providers (International SOS, Global Rescue)
