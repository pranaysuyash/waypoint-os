**Topic**: #27 (Exploration Topics Master Index)

---

## Purpose

Design a mobile-optimized experience for travel agency operators who are not desk-bound. Agents do site visits, airport pickups, client meetings, and work from home. A mobile surface for quick capture, status checks, and follow-up management is essential for field productivity.

---

## Key Questions

- PWA vs native app for mobile operator workflows?
- What is the minimum viable mobile surface? (quick capture, status checks, follow-ups)
- How do we handle offline-first draft capture when connectivity is spotty?
- What role does voice memo intake play on mobile?
- How do push notifications work for SLA breaches, follow-ups, and approvals?
- Quick-capture mode vs full workspace mode — how do we separate the two?
- How do we handle call recording and transcription on mobile?
- What's the cross-device state sync strategy? (mobile capture → desktop workspace)

---

## Research Areas

- PWA manifest and service worker implementation
- Offline-first draft capture with sync and conflict resolution
- Voice memo upload and transcription workflow
- Push notification strategy for agency operators (SLA breaches, follow-up reminders, approvals)
- Mobile-first intake UX: large text input, minimal taps, one-click process
- Role-based mobile surfaces (owner quick view vs agent capture mode vs admin dashboard)
- Phone integration: tap-to-call, contact linking, call log import
- Cross-device state sync (mobile capture → desktop workspace continuation)
- Mobile performance budget and loading strategy
- Biometric authentication for quick re-auth on mobile
