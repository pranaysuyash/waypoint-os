## Purpose

Design the traveler-facing half of the product value chain. The current product focuses entirely on the agency operator, but travelers need booking confirmations, itinerary access, payment capabilities, feedback channels, and communication during their trip.

---

## Key Questions

- Do travelers get a self-service portal to view their trip details and documents?
- How are booking confirmations, invoices, and itinerary documents delivered?
- What's the traveler feedback loop? (satisfaction surveys, trip reviews)
- Should travelers be able to request changes mid-trip? (self-service vs agent-mediated)
- How do we handle traveler-facing content vs internal agency content safely?
- What communication channels should travelers use? (WhatsApp, email, SMS, portal)
- How does the system handle emergency communication with travelers?
- What personalization is appropriate for traveler-facing content?
- How do we handle traveler authentication without adding friction?

---

## Research Areas

- Traveler portal design (itinerary view, document access, payment history, trip timeline)
- Document delivery: booking confirmations, e-tickets, invoices, visa documents, travel insurance
- Payment collection from travelers (payment links, installments, automated reminders)
- Feedback and review collection mechanisms (post-trip NPS, trip-specific surveys)
- Self-service change requests (with agent approval workflow)
- Multi-channel traveler communications (WhatsApp, email, SMS, in-app notifications)
- Post-trip engagement and retention (repeat booking prompts, loyalty programs)
- Traveler identity and authentication (magic links, OTP, social login)
- Content safety: ensuring only traveler-appropriate content is visible
- Accessibility compliance for traveler-facing surfaces (WCAG)
