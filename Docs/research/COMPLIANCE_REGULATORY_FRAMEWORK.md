## Purpose

Research the domain-specific travel regulations that apply across different markets. The existing Security & Compliance topic (#3) covers data security (encryption, PII, auth), but travel has additional regulatory requirements: the EU Package Travel Directive, UK ATOL bonding, consumer protection laws, visa compliance, insurance requirements, and cross-border tax rules.

---

## Key Questions

- Which regulatory frameworks apply by market? (EU, UK, India, Africa, Middle East, Asia)
- What are the requirements of the EU Package Travel Directive? (financial protection, bonding, insolvency coverage, pre-contractual info)
- How do we handle ATOL bonding requirements for UK-market agencies?
- What visa and passport requirement management is needed?
- How do we handle travel insurance recommendation and compliance?
- What cross-border tax and VAT rules apply when an agency in one country sells travel to another?
- What consumer protection laws govern cancellations, refunds, and changes in each market?
- What accessibility requirements apply to traveler-facing surfaces? (WCAG, ADA)
- What data residency requirements apply for travel data in different markets?

---

## Research Areas

- EU Package Travel Directive (PTD): financial protection requirements, pre-contractual information duties, liability for performance, insolvency protection
- UK ATOL bonding and CAA regulations: when is ATOL required, bonding calculation, consumer protection
- India travel agency regulations: TAAI membership, IATA requirements, forex rules
- African market regulatory landscape: per-country variations, emerging regulatory frameworks
- Middle East travel regulations: visa sponsorship, Umrah/Hajj specific requirements
- Visa and passport requirement databases: integration with Timatic or similar services
- Travel insurance: mandatory coverage by destination, recommendation engine, compliance
- Cross-border tax: VAT on travel services (tour operator margin scheme), withholding tax, GST
- Consumer protection: cancellation rights, refund policies, force majeure, price revision rules
- Accessibility compliance: WCAG 2.1 AA standards for traveler-facing surfaces, ADA requirements
- Data residency: where must traveler data be stored for each market? (GDPR, local data laws)
