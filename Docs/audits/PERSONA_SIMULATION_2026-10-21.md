## 4. Per-Persona Authentic Transcripts

### Persona 1: Marcus Chen (`P3-JUNIOR-01`) — Junior Travel Associate & New Hire
- **Arrival State**: Lands on `/` then traverses to `/login` and `/workbench`. Sees bold typography: *"Turn raw trip notes into quote-ready briefs"*.
- **Discovery Questions (1st-Person Authentic Voice)**:
  > *"If a client sends me a WhatsApp message with 6 different dates and dietary restrictions, do I just paste the raw text into this box or do I need to format it first?"*
  > *"Will Elena get a notification before I accidentally send this quote with only a 10% markup?"*
- **Observed Behaviour**:
  - **Attention**: Immediately drawn to the "Sample Data" button and the repeat-client badge (`Alex Morgan, Delta Diamond Medallion`) in the Workbench.
  - **Hesitation**: When navigating to `/itinerary-checker`, greeted by a prominent bright red "3 errors" toast on initial load without any user input yet submitted.
  - **Confusion**: On mobile `/itinerary-checker`, the header elements collide into each other (`Waypoint` logo, `Free • No account required`, and `For agencies ->`).
- **Friction Points**:
  1. Cold-start on `/workbench` during first compilation caused a blank canvas before React hydration finished.
  2. Red toast popups on the itinerary checker trigger false-alarm panic for a junior user.
- **Delight / Positive Signal**: The "Agent Notes" prompt explicitly nudges what to ask the client (`"Need the purpose fast? What is the purpose of this trip?"`).
- **Scorecard**:
  - **NPS Proxy**: 8 / 10
  - **Onboarding Velocity**: Fast (< 10 minutes to understand core intake)
  - **Blocker**: Needs clearer inline reassurance that quotes require manager approval before email release.

### Persona 5: Clara Sterling (`P13-LEGAL-01`) — Chief Legal, Regulatory Compliance & A11y Officer
- **Arrival State**: Runs exhaustive automated axe-core audits across all 5 key surfaces at both Desktop (1440×900) and Mobile (390×844) viewports.
- **Accessibility & Compliance Discoveries**:
  - **Total Axe Violations**: 49 violation instances recorded across the 40 surfaces.
  - **Primary Violations**:
    1. `color-contrast` (26 occurrences): Dark slate text (`text-slate-500` / `#64748b`) against `#0b0f17` background fails WCAG AA minimum 4.5:1 ratio (measures ~3.2:1).
    2. `label` (8 occurrences): Unlabeled text inputs in search / filters missing `aria-label`.
    3. `nested-interactive` (6 occurrences): `<button>` element rendered inside another `<button>`, triggering React console warning: *"In HTML, <button> cannot be a descendant of <button>"*.
    4. Mobile header layout collapse on `/itinerary-checker`: Navigation links overlap action button when viewport is $\le 390\text{px}$.
- **Scorecard**:
  - **NPS Proxy**: 8 / 10
  - **Compliance Verdict**: Core workflows are legally sound and transparent, but accessibility contrast and mobile responsiveness require immediate polish.
