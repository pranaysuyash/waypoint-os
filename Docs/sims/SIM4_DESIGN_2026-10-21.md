## Why eleven doors

Sim #3 closed its register (FND-0308..0321, 11/14 fixed same day; 0315/
0317/0321 closed 2026-10-20) and explicitly queued Sim #4 at
`SIM3_META_AUDIT_AND_PLAN_2026-10-05.md` §1.2 U12 ("doors never sim'd:
quotes (T4), seasons/suppliers/knowledge, payments absence, corporate
policy, insights") and §2 item 4 ("chase SECOND-order: does a garbage
destination actually produce a wrong quote end-to-end? That's the
money-path proof"). The 2026-10-12 council
(`Docs/review/COUNCIL_FULL_REPO_AND_WAVE_AUDIT_2026-10-12.md`) then found
AUD-01..20; most landed by 2026-10-20 but two residuals are still true in
the tree today:

- `traveler_answers` has **zero readers in `src/`** (`rg traveler_answers src`
  = ∅ at design time) — AUD-06 part two (consumption) is unfixed.
- The run path never consults customer memory (`rg CustomerMemory|MemoryStore
  src/orchestration src/decision spine_api/services/pipeline_execution_service.py`
  = ∅) — Sim #3 P7 residual.

Both are registered here as predictions so a FAIL is a finding against a
named expectation and a PASS retires the residual.

**BFF parity (design-time Observed):** the product BFF is deny-by-default
(`frontend/src/app/api/[...path]/route.ts`, `route-map.ts:10`). Probed via
the running frontend before execution: `api/v1/dsr/*`, `api/v1/visa-radar/
check`, `api/v1/corporate/policy-rules`, `api/v1/customers/memory`,
`api/v1/trips/{id}/accounting-export` → **404**; `followups/dashboard` →
200. Each door below therefore carries a parity grade: reachable from the
product or backend-only.

## Grading contract (inherited from Sim #2/#3)

- Verbatim scripts only; every pass/fail ties to a business surface; zero
  credit for "didn't crash"; defects registered through `scripts/findings.py`
  only; predictions anchored with file:line BEFORE execution.
- Truth labels (doctrine §2): Observed / Verified / Inferred / Proposed /
  Unknown. "Unknown→check" predictions name the check.
- Data safety: all writes additive (new drafts/trips/profiles/mandates/docs);
  no TRUNCATE/DELETE; the only deletions are the GDPR sim's own
  self-created artifacts via the canonical erasure endpoint (that IS the
  surface under test).
