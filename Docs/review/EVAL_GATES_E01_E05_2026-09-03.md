## Executive decision

The repository now distinguishes evaluator calibration from product-quality
evidence. Extraction and end-to-end pipeline lanes currently have no
deterministic producer for their document/agent outputs, so their
expected-vs-expected scores remain available to validate comparison logic but
are explicitly marked `actual_source=expected_fixture_mirror`, `evidence_tier=0`,
and `shadow`. They cannot authorize a public surface.

Budget, colloquial, and gap/decision lanes use live deterministic collectors
when available. The gap/decision lane runs all 30 corpus fixtures and reports
the current engine result. After the X-04/X-05/X-06/X-13 correction wave, the
current decision result is 30/30; extraction and pipeline remain shadow because
their independent producers are still absent.

The findings lifecycle gate now recognizes one canonical register and allows
historical companions only when they declare that role. An undeclared register
is treated as canonical (fail-safe), preventing accidental duplicate sources
of current truth.

## Deliberate non-claims and follow-up

- A mirror score is not extraction quality, generalization, or production
  readiness.
- A deterministic local scenario run is not provider, browser, deployed, or
  real-traveler evidence.
- The pre-fix 17/30 result and its 13 failing cases remain historical evidence
  in the prior snapshot/triage record; the current 30/30 result must not erase
  that regression lineage.
- The holdout README is a policy surface, not evidence that a holdout corpus
  exists; seeding it requires fresh variants and independent review.
- Journey smoke in the normal backend test job proves the in-process contract,
  not compose startup, browser rendering, deployment topology, or recovery.
- The findings companion remains useful for historical task discovery but must
  not be used as current status authority.

## Rollback and promotion

The evaluator changes are additive. To promote a lane, first add an independent
collector and evidence-sensitive tests, regenerate the snapshot, and update
the manifest only after the live result meets its threshold. If the collector
is unavailable or its provenance is uncertain, keep the lane shadow; do not
restore expected-vs-expected authority. The findings role metadata can be
reverted only together with an explicit replacement-register decision.
