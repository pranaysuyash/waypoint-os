## 1. Professional Background & Operational Context

Mateo manages agency profitability by capitalizing on supplier wholesale price differentials:

1. **Bedbank Wholesale Spreads**: Direct hotel contract rates ($2,250) often drop in secondary wholesale channels (Hotelbeds at $1,845) weeks after initial booking.
2. **Margin Arbitrage**: Capturing this spread adds pure profit (+$405 per booking) to agency revenue without altering the traveler's room category or service level.
3. **Automated Voucher Swapping**: Swapping supplier vouchers must be seamless, updating hotel confirmation codes and supplier settlement ledgers without manual re-entry.
