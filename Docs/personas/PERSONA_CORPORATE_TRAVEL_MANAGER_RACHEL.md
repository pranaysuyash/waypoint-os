# Persona Profile: Rachel Vance (P4-CORP-01)

**Role**: Executive Assistant & Corporate Travel Coordinator\
**Company**: *Vertex Global Solutions* (B2B Enterprise Fintech, 450 employees, $1.8M annual travel spend)\
**Profile Archetype**: Corporate Operations & Compliance Gatekeeper (`P4-CORP-01`)\
**Experience Level**: 7+ years managing executive itineraries, board meetings, and roadshows\
**Primary Tech Stack**: Concur / Navan, Expensify, Slack, Google Workspace, Amex Corporate Cards\

---

## 1. Core Responsibilities & Workflow

1. **Executive Multi-City Roadshow Assembly**: Coordinates multi-leg business trips for the C-suite and VP of Sales (e.g. NYC $\rightarrow$ London $\rightarrow$ Zurich $\rightarrow$ NYC).
2. **Corporate Policy Compliance Enforcement**: Enforces company travel policy (e.g. Economy for flights <6h, Business for flights $\ge$6h, $450/night hotel limit in Tier-1 cities, mandatory travel insurance).
3. **Out-of-Policy Exception Routing**: Routes exceptions (e.g. Last-minute flight price surge, urgent First Class request for CEO) to Director of Finance / CFO for formal audit sign-off.
4. **Duty of Care & Traveler Tracking**: Tracks traveler safety, emergency contact info, and local risk alerts during high-stakes international travel.
5. **Consolidated B2B Invoicing & Cost Center Billing**: Categorizes all charges against exact department cost centers (e.g. `CC-SALES-402`, `CC-EXEC-101`) for monthly ERP reconciliation.

---

## 2. Key Pain Points & Frustrations with Traditional Tools

- **Rigid Legacy CBTs (Corporate Booking Tools)**: Tools like Concur make complex multi-leg multi-hotel roadshows take hours to book.
- **Manual Email Approval Hell**: Chasing VPs and Finance for out-of-policy approvals over email creates a paper trail nightmare and causes hotel prices to surge while waiting.
- **Blindness to Live Flight Disruptions**: When an executive's flight is delayed or cancelled at 2 AM in Frankfurt, Rachel is either asleep or on hold with airline support for 90 minutes.
- **Uncategorized Ancillary Expenses**: Executive car services, private dining, and conference Wi-Fi get lost in separate receipts.

---

## 3. Success Metrics & Value Criteria

- **Time Saved per Roadshow**: Under 15 minutes to assemble and validate a 3-city executive itinerary.
- **Policy Compliance Rate**: 100% automated enforcement before booking release.
- **Exception Turnaround Time**: Out-of-policy approval requests routed and approved in 1 click via Slack/Web.
- **Duty-of-Care Radar**: Instant visibility of executive location and automated disruption resolution.
