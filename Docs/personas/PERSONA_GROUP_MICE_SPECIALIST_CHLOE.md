# Persona Profile: Chloe Bennett — Group & Destination Wedding Logistics Specialist

**Identifier**: `P5-MICE-01`\
**Role**: Senior Group Travel Director & Destination Wedding Coordinator at *Azure Bespoke Events*\
**Operational Scale**: Manages 15–40 guest destination weddings, multi-family celebrations, and corporate retreats ($120k–$400k gross booking value per event).\
**Primary Tooling Need**: Pareto consensus optimization, automated rooming list allocation, single-supplement calculation, and itemized multi-payer split payment ledgers.

---

## 1. Professional Background & Operational Context

Chloe coordinates high-complexity group journeys where individual travelers have drastically different budgets, rooming preferences, and activity desires. Her core operational bottleneck is the **"Group Consensus Trap"**:

- The couple wants a $1,200/night 5-star cliffside villa in Positano, but bridesmaids have a strict $2,500 total budget.
- Solitary travelers refuse to subsidize couples sharing master suites.
- Chasing 20 separate bank transfers or Venmo payments manually results in cash flow deficits and missed hotel deposit deadlines.

---

## 2. Core Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD)

1. **Harmonic Group Consensus**: Evaluate candidate properties and activity schedules across multi-party preference bounds to find the Pareto-optimal itinerary that minimizes collective dissatisfaction.
2. **Fair-Share Split Payment Ledger**: Automatically generate individualized payment links for all 20 guests with itemized single-room supplements, villa master suite upgrades, and optional activity add-ons.
3. **Automated Deposit Deadlines**: Track payment statuses per guest and auto-trigger polite WhatsApp reminders before supplier attrition lock dates.

---

- **Destination**: Amalfi Coast & Capri (18 Guests).
- **Core Challenge**: Resolving the budget spread between Budget Guest ($2,500 cap) and Luxury Guest ($5,500 cap) while allocating private catamaran charters to Capri and ensuring zero unpaid balance before hotel contract cut-off.
