## 1. Persona Profile & Context

* **Role**: Tech-savvy solo travel curator planning high-touch luxury and cultural itineraries for friends, family, and high-net-worth private clients.
* **Tech Stack**: Notion, Airtable, ChatGPT, Google Docs, TravelJoy, WhatsApp, Apple Notes.
* **Operating Model**: 5–12 active trips per year; high average booking value ($10,000–$25,000 per trip); 100% remote.
* **Mindset**: Modern, design-conscious, values speed and UX polish (admires apps like Linear, Raycast, Superhuman). Skeptical of legacy enterprise GDS software (Sabre/Amadeus) and clunky CRM forms.

## 2. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

1. **Instant Unstructured Ingestion**: Take messy, informal WhatsApp or voice-note snippets and turn them into structured, qualified trip parameters in seconds without manual transcription.
2. **Safety & Compliance Guardrails**: Ensure that critical information (passports, dietary allergies, seasonal advisories, visas) is never overlooked or miscalculated.
3. **Frictionless Follow-Up**: Automatically identify missing information (e.g., origin airport or budget) and draft contextual client questions with 1-click WhatsApp copy.
4. **Margin & Commission Protection**: Prevent margin leakage by tracking supplier split commissions and factoring in FX volatility buffers for international bookings.
5. **High-End Client Presentation**: Deliver client-facing proposals that look bespoke, editorial, and interactive rather than a generic PDF printout.

* **Half-Baked Quotes**: Pressure to reply quickly leading to quotes given without knowing exact party composition or budget constraints.
* **Legacy Software Bloat**: Enterprise travel software requiring weeks of training, certification, and multi-year lock-in contracts.
