## 1. Professional Background & Operational Context

Clara ensures agency proposals never expose the business to breach-of-contract or consumer protection lawsuits:

1. **Multi-Turn Intent Contradictions**: Clients frequently contradict themselves across emails (e.g. morning departure requested in Turn 1, then evening departure stated in Turn 3), causing booking errors if unflagged.
2. **Implicit Duty-of-Care Needs**: When a client mentions traveling with a 6-month-old infant, an intelligent agency must automatically enforce infant bassinet seat locks and block tight connections under 90 minutes.
3. **Hard Exclusions**: Clients with safety anxieties or corporate policies demand total avoidance of specific aircraft models (`Boeing 737 MAX`) or ultra-low-cost carriers (`Ryanair`).
